## What To Say In Outreach

Subject: Aggregated dataset on consent and social voting in a Spanish digital experiment

Short pitch:

White Mirror Lab ran a 92-day micro social experiment with consent, profiles, ranking, voting and sharing. The aggregate dataset covers 45,077 events, 2,253 anonymous identifiers, 2,979 sessions, 309 profiles and 343 recorded profile votes. 71.14% of profile votes were positive. No raw personal data, identifiers, profile URLs, uploaded content or free text are sold.

Useful for:

- Consent/onboarding research.
- Social reputation mechanics.
- Spanish-language digital behavior.
- Early-stage product conversion.
- Desktop-versus-mobile intent.
- Weird/privacy-first product positioning.
