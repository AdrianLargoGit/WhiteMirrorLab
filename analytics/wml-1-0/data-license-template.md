# WML 1.0 Aggregate Dataset License Template

This is a business starting point, not legal advice. Have counsel review before signing.

## Licensed Material

White Mirror Lab licenses only the aggregated files explicitly delivered to the buyer. The licensed material excludes raw PostHog events, personal data, identifiers, uploaded content and session recordings.

## Permitted Uses

- Internal research and analysis.
- Product, UX, market and consent-flow benchmarking.
- Publication of high-level conclusions with attribution to White Mirror Lab, provided no table or chart reveals suppressed small cohorts.

## Prohibited Uses

- Reidentification or attempted reidentification.
- Joining or enriching the dataset with identity graphs, CRM records, device graphs, ad IDs or people-search datasets.
- Individual targeting, profiling or automated decision-making about a person.
- Resale, sublicensing or redistribution without written permission.
- Claiming the dataset contains raw user-level behavioral records.

## Privacy Controls

The buyer must preserve aggregation, maintain access controls, and delete the dataset at the end of the license term if requested. Any public output must keep cohorts at `users >= 20` unless White Mirror Lab approves otherwise in writing.

## Warranty Limits

White Mirror Lab provides the dataset as aggregated research material. It does not guarantee that the dataset is complete, representative of a general population or suitable for regulated decision-making.

- Non-exclusive starter license: EUR 490.
- Research/commercial license: EUR 1,500.
- Exclusive report or custom analysis: EUR 3,000-5,000.
- Term: 12 months.
- Territory: worldwide, subject to applicable law.
