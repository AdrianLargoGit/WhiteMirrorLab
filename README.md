# White Mirror Lab

White Mirror Lab is a social experimentation lab that explores how people behave inside digital systems.

We build temporary, transparent and opt-in applications designed to study collective behavior, online reputation, digital identity and the ethical limits of technology.

## About The Project

This repository contains the official website for White Mirror Lab.

The site presents the lab manifesto, active experiments, public participation flows, legal documentation, creator tools and the WML ecosystem.

## WML 1.0 / Karma Score

WML 1.0 is the first public experiment by White Mirror Lab.

Karma Score studies digital reputation through anonymous positive and negative votes. Participants can create a profile, receive votes, appear in rankings and share their public profile while knowing that the experiment is voluntary, time-limited and governed by clear consent.

## WML X.X.0

WML X.X.0 is a desktop pet widget for Windows.

It reacts to local activity signals, earns points and connects to a creator marketplace where users can discover skins, pets, outfits and visual packs made by independent creators.

## Creator Marketplace

The marketplace lets creators submit skin packs for review and lets users download or buy approved packs for WML X.X.0.

Each pack is designed to be imported into the desktop widget and expand the visual personality of the WML experience.

## Ethics

Every White Mirror Lab experiment is built around informed consent, transparency, time limits and the publication of aggregated learnings.

The goal is not to manipulate users, but to reveal uncomfortable patterns in digital behavior under a clear ethical framework.
